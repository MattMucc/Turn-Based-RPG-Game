## Confirmed direction
- 3D Unity turn-based RPG with multiple party members and distinct character mechanics/playstyles.
- Expedition 33 is an inspiration for timed dodge/parry defense; cinematic QTEs are also desired.

## Accepted battle foundation
- Full grid with eight-direction movement, one point per adjacent cell including diagonals, and no walking through blocked corners. User approved this baseline rather than chess-like traversal restrictions. Prototype world scale is 2 metres per horizontal cell and 1 metre per elevation level; verify scale visually before treating it as final. Ranged line of sight and accuracy remain open.
- Six attributes: Vitality, Strength, Magic, Physical Defense, Magical Defense, Initiative. Vitality is intended to support maximum HP; exact scaling/formulas remain open. Movement is NOT a leveled attribute. Each character gains THREE movement points at the start of their turn, added to their own remaining balance from the previous turn. No carryover cap approved; prototype has none. Future effects may suppress/modify the grant.
- Strength should determine jump height, with a baseline of one elevation level. Exact thresholds, caps and whether temporary Strength modifies traversal are undecided. Do not remove Strength-based jumping without discussion.
- Movement budget, horizontal jump range and Strength-based vertical clearance are separate. Accepted baseline: horizontal range 2 cells, vertical clearance 1 level. A one-cell gap needs a 2-cell jump costing 2 movement; a two-cell gap needs range 3 and costs 3 movement. Jumping spends movement, never an additional action. Strength thresholds are not implemented yet.
- Initial elements: Fire, Lightning, Ice. Initial statuses: Burning, CHILLED (user replaced Frozen), Paralyzed, Weakened, Exposed, Regeneration, Barrier. Chilled reduces movement; amount, stacking and duration remain open. Paralyzed prevents dodging and parrying. Do not infer either status skips turns.
- Burning: stacks represent remaining ticks; at a burn tick, apply burn damage then remove ONE stack. One stack produces one tick; three stacks survive for three ticks unless modified. More applications add stacks. Tick damage formula, source attribution, caps and whether damage also scales with stack count are not finalized; do not assume all stacks are consumed at once.
- Skills can apply multiple stacks on a successful status application. A landed hit is required before rolling a conditional on-hit status chance. Status application probability is separate from hit accuracy. Example explicitly requested: base 10% plus equipment bonus of 5 percentage points gives 15%; display base and modifiers, e.g. `10% (+5%)`, with a clear total and source breakdown. Per-hit vs per-action rolls for multi-hit skills remain open.
- Full carried consumable inventory is available in combat; no separate battle loadout/cap. Using an item costs an action. Example 99 items illustrates access to carried quantity, not a confirmed maximum stack size. Storage should be generous; exact capacity remains open.
- Initial terrain: one hazard and one obstacle. Ranged accuracy based on distance/elevation is under consideration, not approved as a formula. Melee access to distant/elevated enemies needs deliberate encounter design.
- Three active party members; visible order and one turn per combatant per round; Initiative orders turns rather than granting extra turns. Movement NEVER consumes the main action, including longer movement. Movement may occur before and after an action. Actions include attacks, skills, Block/Defend and items. Taking an action does not end the turn. Explicit End Turn control permits ending with unused movement, which carries over. Party switching's previously proposed action cost is still a baseline for later integration. Reactive dodge/parry does not consume the next turn; reliable Block mitigation and explicit timing assistance are desired.
- Other accepted initial recommendations: physical/magical damage classification separate from elemental affinity and applied statuses; explicit skill scaling; no permanent character elemental weaknesses initially; AoE/multi-hit support with controlled trigger frequency; designed breakable parts on selected enemies; later shared synergy gauge with one initial team action before adding tiers; crafting deferred; cinematic QTE outcomes explicitly defined.
- User explicitly intends future statuses, attacks or skills that prevent a turn. Support this in the eventual turn lifecycle; exact skip behavior, status ticking and debt collection during skipped turns remain open. Preserve the agreed opportunity to handle due debt when defining those interactions rather than silently choosing a policy. Status lifecycle must prevent a newly created effect expiring accidentally in the same processing pass.
- User authorized starting the battle-grid prototype. An isolated traversal/action-budget sandbox is now implemented (see below); the full encounter loop and character kits remain future work.

## Accepted reactive defense design
- User accepted dodge and parry staying in the current grid tile. Reactive defense spends neither movement points nor the next turn/action. Actual repositioning is reserved for movement and specific skills; dodge animations must not change logical occupancy.
- Dodge avoids that hit's damage and on-hit statuses, with a more forgiving timing window and no inherent offensive reward. Parry avoids that hit's damage and on-hit statuses with a tighter window and counterattack reward. Exact timing windows remain tunable/undecided.
- Block remains an action chosen on the character's turn, reducing incoming damage until their next turn. Block remains effective after a failed dodge/parry attempt as a safety net. Final mitigation amount remains open; existing prototype numbers are not final balance.
- Enemy attack presentation: commit to a clearly identified target/area, settle camera for readability, recognizable visual/audio wind-up cues, separate defense opportunity per impact, then immediate damage/status resolution. Missing an earlier combo hit must not automatically prevent defense against later hits. Preserve the existing auto-camera preference when implementing presentation.
- Ordinary attacks allow dodge/parry. Explicitly telegraphed heavy attacks may allow dodge but disallow parry; size alone does not imply unparryable. Terrain hazards and ongoing damage such as Burning do not use reactive defense prompts.
- Area attacks use one defense input per impact, evaluated for each affected party member separately. A Paralyzed character cannot dodge/parry even if the input succeeds for eligible allies. Full party/status systems remain unimplemented.
- Initial counter baseline: parry every hit of a targeted combo to earn one counterattack. Counter reach must be respected; melee defenders cannot teleport to distant attackers. Counter damage, unavailable-reach handling, AoE counter ownership and future kit-specific rewards remain open.
- Accepted first prototype scope: one clearly telegraphed single hit and one three-hit combo with adjustable timing assistance. Cinematic QTEs follow after the basic defense feels good, sharing timing infrastructure but keeping their own prompts/outcomes.
- User approved Space / controller B for Dodge and R / controller RB for Parry during enemy attack sequences. Press rather than hold; first attempt commits for that hit, with a fresh chance on each subsequent hit. Simultaneous dodge+parry is treated as a failed conflicting attempt in the prototype. Exact timing/counter numbers remain tuning, not final rules. Implementation is recorded below.
